## Best Practices (from 22nd Century Frontier)

1. **Subject line:** Metric + ask (e.g., "$12.5K MRR seeking $750K pre-seed")
2. **Length:** Under 150 words
3. **Personalization:** Reference their portfolio or thesis
4. **Ask:** Specific time window (not "let me know if you're interested")
5. **Follow-up:** Day 3 and Day 7 if no reply
6. **No attachments:** Links only (deck on Docsend, etc.)
