## Product Description

SquidWeave is an all-in-one, open-source Marketing Operating System (Marketing OS) that replaces 5-7 separate marketing tools with a single unified platform. It handles organic social media scheduling, paid advertising across Google/Meta/LinkedIn, retargeting pixel management, SEO analysis, email drip campaigns, and AI-powered analytics with attribution modeling.

Unlike traditional SaaS marketing tools that cost $500-5,000/month and lock users into proprietary systems, SquidWeave is self-hostable, costs $0-499/month, and gives users complete data ownership.

## Key Features

- **Organic Marketing:** Social media scheduler, SEO analyzer, email drip campaigns
- **Paid Advertising:** Native Google Ads, Meta Ads, and LinkedIn Campaign Manager integration
- **Retargeting:** One-click pixel generation for Meta, Google, LinkedIn, and Twitter
- **AI Analytics:** Attribution modeling, ROAS tracking, predictive insights
- **Automation:** A/B testing, bid optimization, programmatic buying
- **Enterprise:** White-label, multi-account MCC, SSO/SAML

## Founder Bio

SquidWeave was built by a solo technical founder who experienced firsthand the pain of marketing tool fragmentation. After spending $900+/month on HubSpot, Hootsuite, and separate ad managers — while still unable to answer "which ad drove this sale?" — the founder built SquidWeave as the unified marketing platform they wished existed.

The project is bootstrapped, open-source, and community-driven.
