### Post 7: Educational
The 3 metrics that actually matter in paid advertising:

1. ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) — Are you making more than you spend? Target: 3x+
2. CTR (Click-Through Rate) — Do people care about your ad? Target: 1.5%+
3. CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) — What does a customer cost? Target: <30% LTV

Everything else is vanity.

Impressions? Meaningless without clicks.
CPC? Useless without conversion data.
Engagement rate? Nice, but does it drive revenue?

Focus on ROAS, CTR, CPA. Ignore the noise.

#paidads #marketingmetrics #data
