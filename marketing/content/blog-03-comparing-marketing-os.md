## The Marketing Tool Problem

- Hootsuite for social scheduling

- Google Ads for search
- Meta Business Manager for Facebook/Instagram
- LinkedIn Campaign Manager for B2B

**Monthly cost:** $300-800+
**Time spent managing:** 10-15 hours/week
**Data fragmentation:** Complete

There has to be a better way.

3. Retargeting and tracking

5. Automation and optimization

Instead of 5-7 tools, you use one. Instead of $300-800/month, you pay $0-149/month.

A self-hosted Marketing OS like SquidWeave changes the equation:

- **Own your data:** JSON files you control, not a vendor's database

- **Zero vendor lock-in:** Export everything and walk away

**Trade-off:** You need a server (or free hosting like Render, Railway, or Fly.io).

## Migration Guide: Switching from HubSpot/Mailchimp

### Step 1: Export Your Data
- **HubSpot:** Settings > Export data > Contacts, campaigns, workflows
- **Mailchimp:** Audience > Export audience > Campaigns > Export reports
- **Hootsuite:** Analytics > Export > Posts, engagement data

### Step 2: Import to Your New OS
- Upload contact CSVs
- Recreate email templates (or redesign better ones)
- Reconnect ad accounts (Google, Meta, LinkedIn)
- Reinstall retargeting pixels (one-click if your new OS supports it)

### Step 3: Parallel Run (2 weeks)
- Run both platforms simultaneously
- Compare deliverability, open rates, click rates
- Confirm data accuracy

### Step 4: Cut Over
- Cancel old subscriptions
- Redirect old tracking domains
- Update team training
