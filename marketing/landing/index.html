Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free tier is free forever. Upgrade only when you want to connect paid ad platforms.

Do the numbers in the app come from real data?

Yes. Campaign, contact, and funnel figures come from your own data. The SEO analyzer fetches the live page you give it. We don't simulate results.

How do the ad integrations work?

You connect your own Google, Meta, or LinkedIn account with your credentials. SquidWeave verifies the token live before connecting. This is early access — expect rough edges.

Who owns my data?

You do. Data is stored in files you control and can export. If you self-host, it never leaves your infrastructure.
