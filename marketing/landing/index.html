Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a credit card to start?
No. The Free tier is completely free forever. No credit card required. Upgrade only when you need paid ad features.
Can I connect my existing ad accounts?
Yes. SquidWeave connects to your existing Google Ads, Meta Business Manager, and LinkedIn Campaign Manager accounts. We do not hold your ad spend — you pay the platforms directly.
Is there a white-label option for agencies?
Yes. The Enterprise tier includes full white-labeling, custom domains, and multi-account MCC management. Perfect for marketing agencies managing client campaigns.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your data is stored in JSON files that you own. Export everything before canceling, or downgrade to the Free tier to keep read-only access to your historical data.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes. All paid plans have a 14-day free trial. If you are not satisfied after upgrading, contact support within 30 days for a full refund.
