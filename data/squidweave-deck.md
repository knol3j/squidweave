# SquidWeave — Fundraising Brief

## Problem
Founders spend 200+ hours on manual investor outreach with poor tracking, low response rates, and no systematic follow-up. Traditional VC networks are insufficient for pre-seed founders who need broader, automated distribution.

## Solution
SquidWeave is an AI-powered fundraising automation platform that orchestrates multi-channel investor outreach across email, LinkedIn, and social channels. Features include:
- Automated investor scoring and prioritization
- Personalized messaging with dynamic templates
- Reply monitoring and sentiment analysis
- CRM integration and deal stage tracking
- Compliance and approval workflows

## Why Now
- $27B TAM for fundraising tech growing 35% annually
- AI agent technology now mature enough for complex workflows
- Market gap: No existing tools automate full fundraising pipelines
- Regulatory changes around warm introductions creating urgency

## Go-to-Market
- **ICP**: Seed-stage AI companies, solo founders, pre-seed startups
- **Channels**: Email outreach, LinkedIn automation, partner referrals
- **CAC/LTV**: <$500 customer acquisition with $12K ARR target
- **Expansion**: White-label partnerships with VC firms and accelerators

## Traction
- Running live with 3 active campaigns
- 25+ agents orchestrated in single workflows
- Built on composable architecture (open source foundation)
- Early revenue validation from consulting clients
